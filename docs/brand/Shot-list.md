# Berry Brown · Shot list

These photos don't exist yet. Until they do, the brand book uses rose placeholder frames. Put each photo in this folder as soon as it is taken.

## The rules for every photo

- Use a phone camera and window light.
- Shoot on her kitchen counter, with her plates.
- Keep hands in the frame whenever you can.
- Leave small imperfections in. Don't retouch or boost the colours.
- No studio lights, no white backdrop, no ring light, no stock photos, no AI-made cakes.
