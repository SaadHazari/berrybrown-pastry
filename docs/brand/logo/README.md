# Berry Brown · logo files (locked 24 Sep 2026)

## What is locked
- Icon: the strawberry sprig. Seeds: diamond lattice, 13, teardrop. Do not redraw.
- Name: the original hand lettering, traced. Use the file, never retype it.
- Tagline: MADE WITH HEART · NOT HASTE in Jost Medium, all caps, letter-spacing 0.32 em, 86% of the name width.
  The middle dot (·) is the divider in the caps lockup and the seal ring. In running text (website copy, captions,
  Instagram bio) write it as a sentence with a comma: "Made with heart, not haste."
- Spacing: icon to name 30, name to tagline 22 (at the 380-wide master).
- Colours: Cocoa #3E2A21 (ink), Claret #7A2A3A (the berry, one accent), Butter #F6EEDF (background), Rose #E7CFC6 (soft background, stickers).
- Body font for website, price sheet, cards: Alegreya Medium. Small caps and labels: Jost.

## The tab icon (favicon)
It is the berry alone with its locked 13-seed lattice, tilted to fill the square edge to edge, with a thin butter halo around the outline.
The halo is what makes it readable on a dark Safari tab (Safari does not recolour icons), and it disappears
into a light tab. Transparent background. Do not put it in a circle and do not add the stem or the name.

## Rules
- Clear space around the logo: the height of the berry, on every side.
- Smallest full lockup: 72 px on screen, 25 mm in print. Below that use the sprig alone or the seal.
- Never stretch, recolour, add effects, or put the logo on a photo without a butter or rose panel behind it.
- PNGs are transparent except the on-dark set, which carries its cocoa background.
